How to use this tracker

Every day (5 minutes)

  1. Morning: write your Top 3 tasks. Only three.
  2. Evening: fill in each goal: enter the number (minutes, hours, km...) and tick the goal only if you met its rule.
  3. Tick your routine steps, enter your bedtime, and write 1-2 lines of reflection.

The rule for ticking each goal (be honest)

  • Spiritual: at least 15 minutes of prayer or reflection.
  • Education: at least 2 hours of focused study (phone away).
  • Money: spent no more than your daily budget, and only on planned items.
  • Discipline: no more than 5 game matches, and in bed by 22:20.
  • Health: at least 20 minutes of running or exercise.

You can change these rules to suit you, but decide them once and do not change them when a day goes badly. A missed day is data, not failure.

How to measure where you are

  • Consistency = days done ÷ days passed. This is your real score. 80% or more is On Track; 50-79% is Slightly Behind; below 50% is Behind.
  • Time used shows how far you are in the goal deadline (90 days, 16 days). Compare it with days done.
  • Weekly averages (study hours, km, matches, spending) show if the numbers are improving. Compare this week with last week.
  • Daily score = share of goals plus routine steps completed that day.

Where you are going

  • Education: aim for GPA 3.5. Track study hours and exam accuracy weekly; more focused hours with rising accuracy leads to the GPA.
  • Discipline and health: aim for a streak. A 21-day streak means the habit is forming.
  • Money: aim for weeks where actual spending is at or below budget.

Tips

  • Never skip twice in a row. If you miss a day, restart with the smallest version (3 minutes).
  • Do the weekly review every Sunday. This is where progress is actually decided.
  • Your data saves automatically (see the status at the top of Today).
